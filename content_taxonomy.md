# Content Taxonomy

This file defines the classification system used to tag and analyze YouTube videos in the retirement content space. It is used by the AI tagging pipeline and the trends dashboard.

## Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

Every video serves one or more "jobs" for the viewer. These are the core reasons someone clicks on and watches a retirement/finance video. Tag each video with ALL that apply.

| Label | Description | Example titles |
|---|---|---|
| **Emotional Stabilization** | Reduces stress, fear, and uncertainty about money. Reassures the viewer that they're going to be okay. Makes the complex feel manageable. | "You Have Enough to Retire — Here's Why", "Stop Worrying About Social Security" |
| **Explain Concept** | Turns a complex financial system, rule, or strategy into a clear, usable framework. The viewer walks away understanding something they didn't before. | "How COLA Actually Works", "Roth Conversions Explained Simply" |
| **Social Calibration** | Helps the viewer understand where they stand relative to others — income, savings, lifestyle, spending. Answers "am I normal?" or "am I behind?" | "Average Retirement Savings by Age", "How Much Do Retirees Actually Spend?" |
| **Decision Outsourcing** | Gives the viewer a clear recommendation so they don't have to evaluate everything themselves. Answers "just tell me what to do." | "The Only 3 ETFs You Need", "Claim Social Security at THIS Age" |
| **Identity & Community** | Helps the viewer adopt a financial worldview or feel part of a group. Creates belonging around a shared approach to money. | "Why I'm a Boglehead", "The FIRE Movement After 60", "Join the Debt-Free Life" |
| **Incite Fear** | Uses fear, urgency, or alarm as the primary hook. Frames the situation as a crisis. This is an **anti-pattern** for our channel but is important to track because it's common in the space and often drives high view counts. | "Social Security Is COLLAPSING", "You're Running Out of Time!", "The Retirement Crisis Nobody Talks About" |
| **Other** | Doesn't clearly fit the above categories. Could be entertainment, lifestyle, vlog, or tangentially related content. | "Day in My Life as a Retiree", "Moving to Portugal at 62" |

## How to tag

- A single video can have **multiple** JTBD tags. For example, a video titled "Average 401k Balance by Age — And What To Do About It" would be tagged **Social Calibration** + **Decision Outsourcing**.
- A video titled "STOP Making This Social Security Mistake!" could be **Incite Fear** + **Decision Outsourcing** depending on the actual content.
- When in doubt, tag based on what the **title and description promise to the viewer**, not what the video might actually deliver.
- The **Incite Fear** tag should be applied when the title or framing uses urgency, alarm, or catastrophizing as the primary hook — even if the underlying content is reasonable.

## Our channel's alignment

Our channel primarily serves these jobs:
1. **Emotional Stabilization** — our core value prop. "You're not behind."
2. **Explain Concept** — making the complex simple.
3. **Decision Outsourcing** — giving clear, actionable next steps.

We avoid **Incite Fear** entirely. We use **Social Calibration** carefully — framing comparisons as informational, never as judgment.
